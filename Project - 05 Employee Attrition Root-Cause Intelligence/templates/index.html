Star Performer Retention Hazard (The High-Rating Trap)
Critical Risk Segment

Empirical analysis identifies an acute vulnerability pattern termed the "Star Performer Trap":

Observed Hazard Rate: 43.8% Attrition
Employees with Performance Rating 4 or 5 who experience ≥ 2.5 years without promotion and have a compa-ratio < 0.95 exhibit 3.2x higher turnover hazard than baseline.

These high-performing engineers possess high external market liquidity. When promotion cycles bottleneck and compensation lags below market benchmarks, they represent the single highest replacement and client-disruption cost to TCS accounts.
